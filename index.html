How to play

Guess the hidden English word. Words can be any length. Your guess must have the same number of letters as the answer.

  • Green: right letter, right position.
  • Yellow: right letter, wrong position.
  • Grey: that letter isn't needed in the word.

Easy gives you 12 guesses, Normal gives you 6, and Hard gives you 3. There are unlimited rounds.
